Task Creator Guide

How It Works

As a Task Creator, you post AI inference tasks with ETH bounties. Miners compete to complete your task. You get the best result.

  • Describe what you need analyzed
  • Upload your data to IPFS
  • Set a bounty (higher = more miners)
  • Wait for miners to submit results
  • 5 validators verify the best result

Tips for Success

Maximize the quality of results you receive:

  • Be specific in your task description
  • Provide clear data references
  • Set competitive bounties (0.01+ ETH)
  • Allow enough time (24+ hours)
  • Check miner reputation before hiring

Use Cases

What you can use Proof of Inference for:

  • Luxury goods authentication
  • Document analysis & verification
  • Image recognition tasks
  • Data classification
  • Content moderation

Cost & Privacy

Understanding the economics and privacy:

  • You only pay the winning miner
  • Data stays off-chain (IPFS only)
  • Results are verified by consensus
  • Cancel anytime before completion
⚡
Miner Guide

How It Works

As a Miner, you earn ETH by running AI inference tasks. The more accurate your results, the more you earn.

  • Browse open tasks in the Task List
  • Download task data from IPFS link
  • Run AI inference using your preferred model
  • Submit your result with confidence score
  • Win consensus to earn the bounty

Winning Strategy

Maximize your earnings and reputation:

  • Use multiple AI models for validation
  • Be honest about your confidence level
  • Focus on tasks in your expertise
  • Build reputation over time
  • Higher reputation = more task access

AI Tools You Can Use

Any AI model works for inference:

  • OpenAI GPT-4 / GPT-4 Vision
  • Anthropic Claude
  • Google Gemini
  • Local models (Llama, Mistral)
  • Specialized domain models

Earnings & Reputation

Understanding the reward system:

  • Win = Full bounty payment
  • Reputation score: 0-100
  • Win rate affects reputation
  • Higher reputation = priority access
  • Build track record over time
